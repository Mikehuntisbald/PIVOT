\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Single-target scope.}
\method emits at most one box or abstention and does not solve
variable-cardinality multi-target GREC.  The gRefCOCO result is a restricted
single/no-target slice.

\textbf{Hard isolation is not universal.}
The frozen-base controls show recurrent destructive shared updates and a large
Ranking benefit from isolation.  Under identical 100k heads, the adapted trunk removes the
direct parent's large isolation penalty, but isolation remains slightly lower
on Test5 and its FPR95 advantage does not exclude zero.  MM-GDINO also favors
wide sharing despite negative tails.  We claim representation-dependent
interference, not a cosine-sign rule or universal isolation benefit.

\textbf{Domain-dependent calibration.}
FineCops and gRefCOCO support rejection-ordering transfer, but the sealed source
threshold misses its 95\% TPR operating point on both.  The sample-level score
is not a domain-invariant probability.

\textbf{Visual support and coverage.}
Visual-support Admission requires an external exemplar and exact-match
taxonomy.  It covers 95.60\% of FineCops positives.  Category text has broader
coverage but receives an explicit category noun; neither interface is
input-equivalent to a standard full-expression-only method.

\textbf{Exposure caveats.}
FineCops is annotation/task-zero-shot, not image-disjoint; gRefCOCO removes
only Abstention supervision and its images were candidate-generator training
inputs.  Broad MM-GDINO pretraining is not image-disjoint, and e5 is
retrospective.  ARROW does not establish positive REC/P@1 SOTA; Strict
negatives are proposal-covered, not image-global or all-query verified.
