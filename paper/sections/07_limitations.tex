\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Single-target scope.}
\method emits at most one box or abstention and does not solve
variable-cardinality multi-target GREC.  The gRefCOCO result is a restricted
single/no-target slice.

\textbf{Hard isolation is not universal.}
The frozen-base controls show recurrent destructive shared updates and a large
Ranking benefit from isolation.  The strong MM-GDINO control does not reproduce
a deployment benefit from isolation: TN10 creates a heavier negative-gradient
tail, yet capacity-matched wide sharing preserves the same REC and has lower,
less variable FPR95.  We claim representation-dependent interference, not a
law that every responsibility needs disjoint parameters or that cosine sign
alone selects the correct topology.

\textbf{Domain-dependent calibration.}
FineCops and gRefCOCO support rejection-ordering transfer, but the sealed source
threshold misses its 95\% TPR operating point on both.  The sample-level score
is not a domain-invariant probability.

\textbf{Visual support and coverage.}
Visual-support Admission requires an external exemplar and exact-match
taxonomy.  It covers 95.60\% of FineCops positives.  Category text has broader
coverage but receives an explicit category noun; neither interface is
input-equivalent to a standard full-expression-only method.

\textbf{Exposure caveats.}
FineCops is annotation/task-zero-shot rather than fully image-disjoint.  The
gRefCOCO disjoint subset removes only Abstention train/calibration images; all
evaluated images were exposed during candidate-generator training.  The strong e5 trunk is a
retrospective replay whose original training evaluated benchmark splits every
epoch, although its owner matrix was frozen prospectively.

\textbf{Positive ceiling and negative verification.}
\method targets selective reliability and does not establish positive REC or
FineCops P@1 SOTA.  Strict negatives are proposal-covered SAM3+VLM verified,
not image-global or all-query verified.
