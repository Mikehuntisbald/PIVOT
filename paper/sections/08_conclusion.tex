\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\method factorizes Admission, Ranking, and Abstention.  Matched controls show
conditional allocation: isolation protects one representation, while the
same-head parent-to-adapted axis shows that adaptation can remove isolation's REC
cost without establishing rejection superiority.  MM-GDINO likewise supports
sharing despite negative tails.  Conflict is diagnostic, not prescriptive;
rejection ordering also transfers better than a fixed threshold.
