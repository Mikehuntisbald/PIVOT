\subsection{Factorization improves both deployed endpoints}
Relative to the frozen route, ARROW-U2 improves Test5 Acc@0.5 by 2.077 points
(95\% paired image-cluster CI [1.827, 2.347]) and lowers Strict-TN2031 FPR95
by 4.185 points (CI [2.457, 6.199]).  This cumulative comparison includes the
additional supervision used by the factorized heads; it is not an ownership
causal control.  Abstention cannot explain the localization gain: the
Admission+Ranking route and full ARROW-U2 have bitwise-identical boxes and
selected queries.  Admission retains 99.69\% compatible-candidate recall while
reducing the mean comparison set to 69.5 queries.

The detailed validation ladder is supplementary.  Its key diagnosis is that
learned Admission constrains the comparison domain, whereas replacing only the
Ranking expression by a category noun reduces validation Acc@0.5 by more than
ten points and changes 37.85\% of top-1 queries.  Admission defines who may
compete; the complete expression determines which admitted instance wins.

\subsection{Responsibility interference is representation-dependent}
Table~\ref{tab:interference} reports final matched statistics.  On the
frozen-base representation, Shared-Wide is not continuously antagonistic: its
mean cosine is positive.  Nevertheless, 15.5--25.0\% of scheduled probes are
negative in every seed, with minima from $-0.393$ to $-0.685$.  Isolation
removes all 450 tested cross-gradient paths and raises Test5 from 69.000\% to
74.311\%, a 5.311-point gain (CI [4.875, 5.768]).  The 0.689-point FPR95
reduction is not significant (CI [$-0.382$, 1.337], $p=0.139$).  Thus sharing
can damage Ranking after capacity, compute, optimizer-state, decay, update, and
data-order matching, but this replay does not establish a rejection advantage.

Before RefCOCO-specific fine-tuning, Shared-Wide has 45.8\% negative U150
probes (mean $-0.0015$, minimum $-0.179$), yet exceeds Isolated by 0.095 Test5
points (CI [0.061, 0.129]) and 0.056 TestAB points (CI [0.013, 0.104]).
Its FPR95 is also 0.689 points lower; the planned isolation-reduction CI
[$-2.340$, 0.365] crosses zero.  Hard isolation
therefore provides no deployed benefit; the native 53.27\% TestAB further
shows that these heads do not replace task-specific trunk adaptation.

After task-specific tuning, e5 Shared-Wide/Isolated remain at
88.979/88.976\% TestAB, and Shared-Wide has lower FPR95 (83.686/84.211\%).
The e6 PosCtrl/TN10 stress test deepens all-milestone q05 from $-0.248$ to
$-0.470$ (U150 $-0.516$), but both continuations retain a $-0.003$-point
Isolated$-$Shared-Wide TestAB gap (CI [$-0.009$, 0]).

Rejection moves in the opposite direction from the isolation hypothesis.  On
TN10, Shared-Wide reaches 67.093$\pm$0.173\% FPR95 versus
68.653$\pm$1.769\% for Isolated; the preregistered Isolated reduction is
$-1.559$ points (CI [$-2.925$, $-0.304$]).  Thus a negative gradient tail is
a useful warning but not a sufficient topology-selection rule.  With separate
task optimizer states, the wide shared owner absorbs these probe conflicts
without REC collapse and is more stable for rejection.  The combined evidence
rejects both extremes: Ranking and Abstention are not inherently antagonistic,
yet matching capacity does not prevent destructive sharing on every
representation.

\subsection{Endpoint accuracy hides Admission responsibility}
The capacity-matched cue routes are close on Test5 but diverge under direct
intervention (Tab.~\ref{tab:admission}, Fig.~\ref{fig:external}).  Learned-null
Admission retains 73.83\% Test5 yet has 0.00\% bidirectional category-switch
success.  Category text reaches 32.29\%, and visual support reaches 48.24\%.
Visual-over-text is 15.95 points (CI [10.81, 20.83]); text-over-null is 32.29
points (CI [28.65, 36.00]); both Holm-adjusted $p=0.0004$.  One exact-support
FineCops surface repeats the ordering.  Visual examples therefore strengthen
category control beyond the name, but are not required for every correct
standard localization.

\subsection{External ordering transfers; calibration does not}
On FineCops, the local original GroundingDINO-T replay has higher positive P@1
than the frozen-base routes, while the learned Abstention score has stronger
negative separation.  Adding Ranking+Abstention changes positive P@1 from
43.11\% to 43.02\%,
raises negative-expression/image Recall@1 from 42.66/43.98\% to 43.85/44.76\%,
and raises official-scope AUROC from 58.87/59.55\% to 60.88/60.69\%.  This is
an improvement in selective discrimination, not positive-grounding SOTA.

On restricted gRefCOCO Full, Abstention raises AUROC from 68.95\% to 71.75\% and
lowers domain-relative FPR95 from 74.10\% to 70.83\%.  On the
Rejector-supervision-disjoint slice, AUROC rises from 68.69\% to 71.50\% and
FPR95 falls from 74.97\% to 71.16\%; planned paired intervals exclude zero.
Because candidate-generator training saw all images, this tests no-target decision transfer rather
than a new image domain.
