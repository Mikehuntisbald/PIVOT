\section{Scope and evidence hierarchy}
\label{sup:scope}

This supplement accompanies the single-target selective-grounding study in
the main paper.  The deployed policy emits one box or abstains; it does not
claim variable-cardinality multi-target grounding.  All manuscript numbers
are materialized from the committed semantic-number registry.  Each registry
entry records its evaluation surface, evidence status, source artifact,
source hash, and, where applicable, confidence interval and seed values.

The evidence hierarchy distinguishes validation-only mechanism analysis,
preregistered held-out contrasts, prospectively frozen post-release analyses,
and external transfer.  A result from one surface is never silently promoted
to another.  In particular, validation splits are not part of the Test5
localization endpoint, and the nested strict-negative subset is not treated as
an independent replication.

All checkpoint, margin, queue, and operating-threshold decisions were sealed
before their corresponding held-out or external forward pass.  Failed runs
and preregistration amendments remain in the artifact lineage rather than
being overwritten by the final summaries.

\paragraph{Legacy-to-paper naming.}
The immutable pre-ARROW schema namespace and internal lineage IDs remain
bound in the release manifest; the paper-facing method is ARROW.  A5 denotes the learned Admission owner, C3 the
clean isolated Rejection objective, D3 its proposal-covered training source,
and O3 the complete exclusive-owner phased system.  O0/O1/O2 denote,
respectively, a shared scalar, separate outputs with a shared trainable
feature, and exclusive owners with interleaved exposure.  The implementation
provider IDs \texttt{AR\_A\_PATCH}, \texttt{AR\_B\_TEXT}, and
\texttt{AR\_C\_NULL} are reported as ARROW-V, ARROW-T, and ARROW-N.  Historical
checkpoint bytes, absolute paths, and schema strings are not renamed.

\paragraph{Hash provenance.}
The release manifest binds the three main checkpoints, legacy ABI, and clean
lineage.  The preregistration, manuscript-facing table receipt, final receipt,
zero-training receipt, and every external result object are independently
SHA-256 bound in \texttt{paper/data/source\_registry.json}.  Generated table
CSVs then map every displayed scalar to a semantic registry key, source path,
source hash, JSON path, surface, and evidence status.
