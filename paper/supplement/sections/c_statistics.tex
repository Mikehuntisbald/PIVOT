\section{Metrics and paired inference}
\label{sup:statistics}

Localization uses expression-level Acc@0.5 or the benchmark-defined P@1.
Rejection uses AUROC, positive-class AUPR, and FPR95.  Confirmatory uncertainty
is computed with paired cluster bootstrap: an image draw carries all of its
expressions and is applied jointly to the baseline, candidate, and all three
training seeds.  Fine-grained counterfactual data use their sealed parent
image as the cluster.  Per-seed metrics are recomputed first and then averaged
with equal seed weight.

FPR95 and fixed-threshold transfer answer different questions.  In every
bootstrap replicate, FPR95 recomputes each model's threshold from that
replicate's positive fifth percentile.  In contrast, fixed-threshold metrics
always reuse the seed-specific threshold sealed on the internal calibration
surface; it is never recomputed or fit on an external benchmark.  Planned
contrast families use Holm correction.  Three-seed summaries report each
seed, the arithmetic mean, and sample standard deviation rather than a
three-sample parametric significance test.

All reported cluster intervals use 5,000 PCG64 replicates.  The frozen
internal/Test5 and ownership contrasts use RNG seed 20260719; the fresh
Admission panel uses 20260818; FineCops-Ref uses 20260819; and gRefCOCO uses
20260820.  Admission, rejection/data, and ownership hypotheses are corrected
within their preregistered families by Holm's step-down procedure; secondary
mechanism and subgroup intervals are descriptive and never used for model
selection.
